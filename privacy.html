Privacy

Owambe Survivor: Naira Rain · owambe.pages.dev

On your device

Your progress (bank, outfits, boosts, streak, level, settings) is saved in your browser so you can continue where you stopped.

Leaderboards and syncing

When online features are on, you get an anonymous account without email or password. If you choose a party name, it appears on the leaderboard with your scores. If you link devices, your progress is stored with that anonymous account so your phone and laptop share it.

Anonymous activity

We record anonymous game activity to improve the game: visits, where the visit came from (for example WhatsApp or Instagram), parties started and finished, scores, dance-offs, purchases with in-game naira, shares, and whether you're on a phone. This uses a random ID stored in your browser, not your name, phone number or contacts.

Google Analytics

We may use Google Analytics, which uses cookies to count visits and see how the game is used. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Featured MCs and DJs

MCs and DJs appear in the game with their permission.

Questions

Want your data removed? Contact the game's creator through the page where you found the game, and include your party name.
