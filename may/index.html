How it works

💁🏾‍♀️ Each client tells you what she wants at the top.

🫧 Wash: shampoo, rinse, towel, blow-dry and comb.

✂️ Cut: drag the scissors across her hair. Lines show short, medium and long.

💇🏾‍♀️ Style: rub a tool on her hair to change it.

🎨 Colour: paint the dye on like a colouring book.

🎀 Extras: tap on her hair to add a bow, flower and more.

📸 Tap Done to take her photo. Every wish you get = ⭐. Stars unlock new colours and goodies.
